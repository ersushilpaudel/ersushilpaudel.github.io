01Research

PhD candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the Laboratory for Advanced Power and Energy Systems (LAPES), Georgia Southern University. My research is nonlinear and adaptive control of power electronic converters — DC-link voltage regulation in VSC-based AC/DC systems, modular multilevel converters and solid-state transformers — taken from Lyapunov-based design through simulation to hardware-in-the-loop validation.

DC-bus voltage regulation

Control strategies for the DC link in VSC-based AC/DC systems, covering two-level converters, modular multilevel converters (MMCs) and solid-state transformers (SSTs).

Non-minimum-phase dynamics

Characterising the right-half-plane zeros of DC-bus dynamics and how they constrain controller design and achievable bandwidth.

Nonlinear & adaptive control

Lyapunov-based controllers — barrier Lyapunov function (BLF), neuro-adaptive (RBF neural network) and prescribed-performance control (PPC) — for nonlinear, uncertain and disturbance-prone conditions.

HIL & PHIL validation

Controllers taken from MATLAB/Simulink into hardware-in-the-loop and power-hardware-in-the-loop experiments at LAPES — RTDS NovaCor with Spitzenberger & Spies amplifiers.
