01Research

PhD student in Electrical Engineering specialising in power electronics, AC/DC systems and grid-connected converters. My work spans modelling, simulation and control of voltage source converters and power system analysis — carried out alongside research collaborations with European and Asian universities and industry partners.

DC-bus voltage regulation

Control strategies for the DC link in VSC-based AC/DC systems, covering two-level converters and modular multilevel converters (MMCs).

Non-minimum-phase dynamics

Characterising and taming the right-half-plane behaviour that limits achievable bandwidth in DC-bus control loops.

Barrier Lyapunov control

A stability-aware barrier Lyapunov function controller for DC-bus regulation in highly nonlinear, uncertain and disturbance-prone conditions.

Real-time validation

Hardware-in-the-loop testing on RTDS NovaCor with grid emulation through Spitzenberger & Spies amplifiers.
